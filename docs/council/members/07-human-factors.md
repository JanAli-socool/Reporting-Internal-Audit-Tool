# Human Factors Specialist

## P1
Keep the button close to where its result appears. A sidebar CTA with the result 1,000px away causes "did anything happen?" moments (re: 02-web-designer.md, which agrees).

## P2
Red-on-red is a signal-detection problem: when everything is salient, nothing is.

## P3
Treating cost as an inverse metric is correct but invisible: "Cost ↑ (red)" works only because the tile says "Operating cost". Keep the labels explicit.

## P5
Warnings must be actionable and specific, not generic yellow banners (re: 03-product-designer.md).

## P8
A spinner is fine under about 10 s. Above that, people need step-by-step progress. The p95 here is about 15 s, so use steps.
